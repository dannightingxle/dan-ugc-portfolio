      Skool

      Where things live.

      Skool has a few tabs along the top. You'll mainly use two of them: Community for posts and Classroom for courses.

      CommunityThe feed. Posts, wins and questions. You can pin posts so they stay at the top, and that's the first thing new members see.
      ClassroomThe courses. Each one is a card that opens into lessons, which is everything below.
      CalendarLive calls. Add a weekly Q&A here and members get reminders.

      Pinned in Community

      Two posts, pinned. Put the same videos in Start Here as well, so people can still find them once the feed gets busy.

        Setting it up

        Building it in Skool.

        1. Pin the two postsPost them in Community, then use the post's menu to pin them.
        2. Add every courseClassroom, then Add course, in the order above. Description, cover, access set to All members, published.
        3. Trailer first, locks afterAdd the trailer as the first page. Add a page for every other lesson with 🔒 at the start of the title and the coming-soon text. Keep them published, because draft pages are hidden from members.
        4. Swap in the videoWhen a lesson is filmed, add the video, remove the 🔒, replace the text and post in Community that it's live.

        If the group is paid, non-members can't see the classroom. Post the free A to Z somewhere public as well and link to it. The waitlist page still lists the original course names and says Course 1 opens on launch day, so it needs a quick update to match this.
